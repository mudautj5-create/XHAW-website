OUR STORY

Created for the love of the outdoors.

Adventure Escape SA was founded in 2024 by Liam Daniels, an outdoor adventure and eco-tourism enthusiast.

He noticed how difficult it could be for families, travellers, schools and corporate groups to compare outdoor experiences in one place. Adventure Escape SA is designed to make discovering packages, selecting activities and calculating estimated booking fees easier.

The business concept focuses on professionally guided outings and the natural beauty of the Western Cape, South Africa.
